Hey Rift friends,

Big week on Collection-Bound. You can build decks right next to your collection now, the tracker works as an app on your phone, and the holiday sale is on.

**Collection-Bound: new Decks tab**

- Build decks, or import one by pasting a list or dropping in a .txt or .json file. Export them any time.
- Everyone gets 3 decks for free. Club gets 15, plus warnings for cards you're missing and a priced shopping list. VIP is unlimited.
- Club and VIP can send a share link. Whoever opens it sees the deck checked against their own collection and can save it.
- Club and VIP also see what a deck is worth today and how that's changed. VIP gets the cheapest language to buy each missing card in, and how much that saves.

Open the Decks tab here: https://unravelingtherift.com/collection-bound/tracker

**Collection-Bound on your phone**

Tap the Get the app button on the tracker for quick install steps for iPhone and Android, or open https://app.unravelingtherift.com in Safari or Chrome and add it to your home screen. It isn't in the App Store or Google Play yet. Sign in with the same account and your collection is right there, with the Starter Handbook, Lore and the Play Simulator in the bar at the top.

**Holiday sale**

Club is $3 a month and VIP is $10 a month until Dec 26 at 11:59 PM Pacific. After that Club goes back to $5 and VIP monthly moves to $12. VIP yearly is now $120 (was $150).

Join here: https://unravelingtherift.com/membership-page
